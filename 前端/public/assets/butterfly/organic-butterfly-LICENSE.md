The project copy removes the source scene's backdrop, camera, and lights. Its animation is trimmed to the articulated wings, abdomen, antennae, chest, and neck for use in the mist hall theme. The derived v2 model adds skinned, softly curved hindwing extensions that reuse the original wing skeleton. The runtime applies an original pearl material and separate company flight behavior.
